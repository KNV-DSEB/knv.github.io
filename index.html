01 — Ships it

Systems that run in production and don’t fall over

Daily jobs with real upstream failure: late files, malformed sources, and models that answer confidently when they shouldn’t.

Daily Market-Intelligence PipelineVietnam Petroleum Institute

Eight sources — Word and PDF market reports arriving by email, price websites, IMF and EIA APIs — consolidated into one AWS RDS PostgreSQL warehouse that produces the daily management report and the dashboard marts behind Apache Superset.

A four-stage orchestrator isolates failure per stage. When a source file is late, the report still ships on the newest data available and marks the lag, rather than silently printing yesterday’s number as today’s.

Python · PostgreSQL · AWS RDS · Apache Superset · star-schema marts · data-quality gates

In production

Internal repository — not public.

A currency mix-up once let prices quoted in USD per tonne into a series measured in dong per kilo. Nothing reaches the warehouse now without passing a pre-write quality gate — which exists because of that bug, not before it.

Grounded LLM Report EngineVietnam Petroleum Institute

The daily report’s written commentary is generated with Amazon Bedrock Nova. Numeric guardrails require every figure in the generated prose to trace back to a row in the warehouse; a second guard blocks invented organisations in attributed forecasts.

Output is persisted back to the warehouse, so the report and the dashboard cannot drift apart and quote different numbers for the same day.

Amazon Bedrock Nova · retrieval grounding · numeric & entity guardrails · PostgreSQL persistence

In production
The guardrails constrain numbers and named entities — not whether an interpretation is any good. A person reads it before it goes out, and that step is not optional.

RAG Translation & Interpreter Training AssistantPersonal system

Document RAG with source-cited answers, a research agent, bilingual terminology extraction, and a mock-interpreting simulator with speech in and out plus feedback-based scoring.

Deliberately built without LangChain, so every outbound call passes one auditable egress layer that blocks when a document is marked confidential. The whole thing runs inside a hard 7.8 GB RAM, CPU-only budget.

FastAPI · ChromaDB · multilingual sentence-transformers · Gemini Flash · faster-whisper · SQLite · vanilla JS

In development github.com/KNV-DSEB/RAG_for_translation
Single-user local software. I have never benchmarked retrieval quality against a labelled set, so I can tell you what it does and not how well it does it.

02 — Decides with it

Turning data into a decision someone can act on

Analytics where the output is a choice — cut this segment, accept this match, price this exposure — and not a chart.

Loan Default Risk DashboardDatapot NextGen Analytics Challenge 2025 · Top 6

Five Power BI pages over a 255,347-loan portfolio carrying 32.6bn exposure at an 11.6% default rate: portfolio overview, borrower profiling, risk lift across credit-score × DTI segments, key-influencer analysis, and a credit-policy simulator.

The influencers came out sharp — the lowest income band at 2.4× default likelihood, borrowers under 25 at 2.2×. The simulator turns a proposed policy into the four numbers a credit committee actually argues about: loans cut, defaults avoided, exposure reduced, expected loss saved.

Power BI · DAX · Power Query · star schema · drill-through · row-level security

Top 6
A static loan snapshot with no time dimension. That rules out DPD buckets, roll rates and vintage curves — so I built none of them and showed none of them, rather than faking a time axis.

InsightX Market CopilotWorking MVP

Explainable product matching and market intelligence across separate Vietnam and Indonesia marketplace data, delivered as a five-page Streamlit product.

19 automated tests and 10/10 page-market smoke checks. Of 15 candidate product pairs that went to human review, 4 were accepted — which is the number I report, because it is the one a person actually verified.

Python · Streamlit · embeddings · heuristic matching · pytest

MVP github.com/KNV-DSEB/InsightX
Heuristic match coverage is not model accuracy and I never report it as one. No pricing claim goes out without verified cost and margin, and VND and IDR never go on the same axis.

Climate Risk Layer for Agricultural LendingPortfolio piece

Typhoon Yagi flood extent mapped from Sentinel-1 radar and overlaid on cropland and commune boundaries, to expose the correlated default risk that PD models assuming independence miss entirely — one storm, one province, every borrower at once.

The output sets a financial-only view of a book against a financial-plus-flood view. 37 unit tests.

Sentinel-1 SAR · Google Earth Engine · geopandas · Python · pytest

Portfolio github.com/KNV-DSEB/Climate-Risk-Layer
The flood is real radar. The loan portfolio is synthetic, and every single chart says so on its face. This is an exposure layer, not a validated PD model — I claim no default-prediction accuracy anywhere.

03 — Questions it

Asking whether the data can support the claim at all

Four projects where the honest finding came out smaller than the one I set out to make — including one that deleted my own headline result.

MRV Carbon Monitoring PipelinePortfolio piece

Sentinel-2 optical and Sentinel-1 SAR acquisition, cloud handling, zonal spectral features and SAR backscatter, plus a data-density reconnaissance step — run on live Google Earth Engine data over a 2.03 km² rice-paddy area in Bac Ninh.

The reconnaissance step exists because of what it found: 0 of 11 usable optical scenes across the rainy season. That is why Sentinel-1 SAR was added as an independent physical cross-check rather than as a nice-to-have. The Streamlit demo runs without Earth Engine credentials.

Google Earth Engine · Sentinel-1 & 2 · Streamlit · Python · geospatial feature engineering

Portfolio github.com/KNV-DSEB/mrv-carbon-monitoring-pipeline
My cloud metric read a flawless 1.000 on every scene. That was the bug. Fixing it deleted my best result — the peak I had been pleased with was a cloud. It detects crop-season phases, not AWD irrigation events, and with no field ground truth I state no accuracy figure anywhere.

Oil-Price Forecasting — a negative resultVietnam Petroleum Institute · research prototype

Walk-forward backtesting, GARCH-MIDAS on mixed frequencies, and a news index built from the report archive the daily pipeline was already collecting.

The finding: price levels behave close to a random walk, and the model did not beat a naive baseline. Whatever value is here sits in volatility and density forecasting, not in the point forecast.

GARCH-MIDAS · walk-forward backtesting · mixed-frequency data · news indexing

Research
It did not beat a naive baseline. I wrote that up and reported it, rather than quietly reframing the question until something won.

Silent Exit — E-commerce ChurnCourse research, extended individually · IEEE format

An eleven-module pipeline running from a recency-based churn label all the way to a retention budget: 29 RFM and behavioural features, Logistic Regression against Random Forest with SHAP, revenue and CLV at risk, and a 664-customer priority segment.

The discount-trap hypothesis — that heavy discount users churn faster — was reported as not supported.

scikit-learn · SHAP · RFM feature engineering · CLV modelling

Paper github.com/KNV-DSEB/Group5_DDM_Final_Project
The 8,000-customer, 25,858-order dataset is synthetic, and the churn label is engineered from a feature the model is later asked to predict it by. That inflates the headline accuracy, so I do not quote the headline accuracy.

Geopolitical Risk & Vietnamese Gold VolatilityIndividual research paper

ARX–EGARCHX on 2010–2025 Vietnamese gold returns, with the Caldara–Iacoviello geopolitical risk index entering both the mean and the variance equation, and positive and negative shocks separated to test asymmetry.

Bai–Perron endogenous break detection replaced the hand-chosen sub-periods the group version had used, with 250-day rolling windows behind it.

EGARCH · ARX · Bai–Perron structural breaks · rolling-window estimation

Paper
The risk index is monthly and carried across trading days, which is a real weakness in the design. This shows association under a specified model, not identified causation — and it is not peer-reviewed.

04 — About

About me

I am a final-year Data Science in Economics and Business student at the National Economics University in Hanoi, and a machine learning intern at the Vietnam Petroleum Institute. Most of what I know about building systems I learned by having one break at eight in the morning while somebody was waiting for the report.

I played competitive chess for sixteen years and won a national youth medal. Chess gave me the habit this whole site is built around: after a game you annotate it, and the annotation that matters is not the brilliant move, it is the one marked ?! — the move that looked strong and wasn’t. Analysing your own losses honestly is the entire skill. It transfers directly to a metric that reads 1.000 and shouldn’t.

I still teach it. I run chess classes at three Hanoi primary schools, to groups of fifteen to fifty children aged six to ten, and I tutor mathematics one to one. Explaining to a seven-year-old why their plan failed, without letting them stop wanting to play, is harder than any technical writing I have done — and it is the reason I can explain a model to a room that did not ask for one.

Alongside that I co-founded a small food venture that reached around 100 units sold and roughly VND 30M in revenue over three months, which taught me what a number looks like when it is your own money.
